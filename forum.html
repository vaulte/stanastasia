Infernal Flora in Temperate Wetlands

Margaret Hale • Department of Botany • 1978
Peer Reviewed Research Paper Restricted Collection

Abstract

This paper documents the discovery and classification of several unidentified wetland flowering species recovered from isolated marshes throughout northern Ohio between 1972 and 1977. Although superficially similar to native aquatic flora, microscopic examination revealed numerous anatomical structures not presently recognised within any known terrestrial taxonomy. Continued observation suggests the specimens exhibit accelerated growth following prolonged exposure to ritual sites associated with historical occult activity.
