The death of Claudius Miéville

Death of St. Anastasia’s University student…
Yesterday, the body of St. Anastasia’s University student, Claudius Miéville, was found by his twin sister in their family home after a weekend away…
https://archives.stanastasia.edu/search

One of our most well-known students, Claudius Miéville, was found dead last night in his apartment by his sister. We ask that in this time you keep his family and friends in mind. The university will be holding a memorial service next Tuesday in his honour, classes will be suspended on that day and any exams rescheduled to accommodate the service. Flowers can be sent to the next of kin by contacting student services and speaking with Marielle Auvray. Please do not contact any family members directly unless you are already in communication with them as we wish to respect their privacy at this time.
