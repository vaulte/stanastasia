An Analysis of Oceanic Motifs in the Cultural Mythology of Saint Anastasia

Claudius F. Miéville • Department of Anthropology • 1998
Peer Reviewed Research Paper Restricted Collection

Abstract

This paper documents the timeline of Saint Anastasia’s association with oceanic imagery and motifs from before she was first canonised as a Saint to modern day local perception. It follows how the association was formed through her death site and burial to the 17th century salt degraded statutes to the modern day altars built on the coastline. Documentation is done through primary sources by interviewing descendents with closed cultural practices and what is being done to keep them alive without losing the origin and meaning of their practices.
