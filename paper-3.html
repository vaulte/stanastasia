The present danger of Null’s to the safety of the Eight Houses

Claudius F. Miéville • Department of Esoteriology • 2000
Research Paper Restricted Collection

Abstract

The most prominent and constant threat to the Eight Houses is exposure to the outside world. Esotericists pose the least likely threat to reveal the Eight Houses as an active participant of the culture, however, Null’s are privileged in their access to the world but denied power. Elimination of Null’s from our society and culture is the only way to ensure our continued survival and safety.
