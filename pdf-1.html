ST. ANASTASIA’s UNIVERSITY
DEPARTMENT OF MARINE BIOLOGY

COASTAL SURVEY REPORT

GALVESTON BAY REGION

October 1978

This document contains the findings of the coastal survey conducted by the Department of Marine Biology during the autumn field period.

Survey operations commenced on October 4 and continued through October 17. Personnel were assigned to multiple observation stations throughout the western bay.

1. OBSERVATION AREA

The primary observation area consisted of approximately 14 square kilometres of coastal water and adjacent marshland.

Visibility during the first week was poor due to persistent fog and unusually low surface temperatures.

2. FIELD OBSERVATIONS

Several observations were recorded outside the expected parameters for the region.

FIELD NOTE: Unidentified movement observed approximately 300m offshore at 03:17 hours.

Personnel were unable to establish the source of the disturbance.

Further investigation was postponed pending review by the department head.

3. SPECIMEN RECORDS

No specimens were retained from the observation period.
